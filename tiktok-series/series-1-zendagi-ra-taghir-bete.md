# Series: «زندگی‌ته تغییر بتی» (8 parts, one per day)

Source: Dan Koe, "How to fix your entire life in 1 day" (X article, Jan 12 2026).
All scripts are written in spoken Dari, in your style: dark room, hoodie, deep calm voice, short lines, pauses, about 25-35 seconds.

**Every video:**
- On-screen text in the first second: `قسمت ۱` + the hook line
- Dari subtitles for every line, placed high (safe area)
- `...` = pause about 1 second
- Last line on screen: «فردا، قسمت بعدی.» (so people follow for the series)
- Pin a comment with the question from the caption
